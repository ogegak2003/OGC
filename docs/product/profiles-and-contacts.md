# Profiles, contacts and sharing

How people present themselves on OGC and find each other (Phase 2).

## Your profile

- **OGC ID** — permanent and random; the way people find you.
- **Display name** — any name you like; not unique (two people can both be "Ada").
- **Photo and bio** — optional. Photos are cropped to a square and stripped of hidden data
  such as location.
- Nothing else is asked for: no phone number, address, birthday or location.

## Privacy controls

| Setting                              | Options (default **bold**)                   |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Who sees my photo, bio and join date | **My contacts** · Everyone                   |
| Who can find me by OGC ID or QR      | **Anyone with my OGC ID** · Only my contacts |
| Who can send me contact requests     | **Anyone who can find me** · Nobody          |

Your email address is never shown to anyone.

## Finding people and adding contacts

1. Get someone's OGC ID — they tell you, share a link, or show their QR code.
2. Enter it in **Discover** (spaces and lower case are fine) or open their link.
3. Review their profile and choose **Add contact**. Nothing happens automatically.
4. They accept or decline. If you both send requests, you become contacts straight away.

Declined requests disappear quietly; you can ask again after seven days.

## Sharing your OGC

"Share my OGC" sends your display name, OGC ID, and a link to your profile. Your QR code
contains only `ogc://id/<your OGC ID>`. Neither ever contains passwords, sign-in data, or
private details.

## Blocking and reporting

- **Block** someone to remove them from your contacts and stop them finding you or sending
  requests. They are not told; to them you simply cannot be found. You can unblock at any
  time (they do not become a contact again automatically).
- **Report** someone for spam, harassment, impersonation, inappropriate content, scams, or
  something else. Reports are confidential and go to OGC moderators.

## Not in this phase

Messaging, groups, channels, calls, phone-number discovery, contact-book upload, usernames,
and a moderation console are not part of Phase 2.
